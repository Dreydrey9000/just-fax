Checked 2026-09-24. Every claim below was re-checked against sources I opened myself: the LOC authority JSON, Wikidata, Open Library ISBN/works APIs, Internet Archive metadata, Simon & Schuster book pages, Amazon product pages, the GKIC page (nobsbooks.com), magneticmarketing.com, Gray & Co and the Harness Museum. All wording is my paraphrase. Short quoted titles are bibliographic facts.

Status key: CONFIRMED = independently re-verified. CORRECTED = mostly right, with the fix stated. UNVERIFIED = I could not confirm it. REFUTED = shown wrong.

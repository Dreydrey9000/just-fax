# Dan Kennedy: bio and alive-or-dead status (fact-checked)

Checked on 2026-09-24 by an adversarial fact-checker. Every source below was reopened and read from raw page text (curl/urllib, or firecrawl when a site blocked plain requests). Summarizer output was not trusted. All claims are paraphrased.

**Headline:** No death evidence exists as of 2026-09-24. The Library of Congress gives his birth date as 1954-12-22. The persona file's "1948-2023" span is wrong on both ends. The newest dated item: a press release from May 19, 2026 says Kennedy would appear live on Day 5 (May 22, 2026) of a free virtual event he co-hosted with Russell Brunson. That is an announcement, not proof the appearance happened.

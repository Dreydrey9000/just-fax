# Gen Z translation lane: adversarial fact-check (2026-09-24)

Checked by re-opening sources with WebFetch, Firecrawl, and local PDF text extraction (pdftotext). All wording is paraphrased. Status key: CONFIRMED, CORRECTED (fixed wording given), UNVERIFIED, REFUTED.

Note on method: the WebSearch budget ran out partway through, so independent checks were made with Firecrawl search/scrape and by opening the primary pages directly. Where a figure was seen only in a search headline and not on an opened page, that is flagged.

## FTC rules and enforcement

- **[CONFIRMED]** GZ-FTC-01: The FTC announced its final Rule on the Use of Consumer Reviews and Testimonials (16 CFR Part 465) on Aug 14, 2024, by a 5-0 vote, and it took effect Oct 21, 2024.

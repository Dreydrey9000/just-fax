# Fact-check: frameworks-business lane (Dan S. Kennedy)

Checked 2026-09-24 by an adversarial fact-checker. Each claim was re-opened at the source and, for high-stakes items, cross-checked with a second source. Everything is paraphrased. Note on tools: the session's WebSearch budget was already used up, so checks ran through Firecrawl search/scrape, direct HTTP fetches (curl) and the Library of Congress JSON record.

Status key: CONFIRMED = matches what the source says. CORRECTED = the substance holds but a detail was wrong or needed tightening (fixed wording given). UNVERIFIED = could not confirm on a page I opened. REFUTED = the source says otherwise.

## Price strategy

The co-author credit is inconsistent across sources.
- **[CORRECTED]** FB-PRICE-02: The book is built around nine "price and fee failures". The description names three of them: worrying too much about competitors' lower prices, attracting customers who buy on price, and not offering premium-priced options. - **[CONFIRMED]** FB-PRICE-03: In "The Dark Side of Discounts" (Entrepreneur.com, Aug 1, 2011, bylined Kennedy and Marrs), the authors say discounts can wreck price integrity fast and shift the buyer's attention from value to price. - **[CONFIRMED]** FB-PRICE-04: "Three Ways to Raise Prices Without Losing Customers" (Entrepreneur.com, June 6, 2011, Kennedy and Marrs) gives three levers: target more affluent buyers, become the recognized leader in your field, and upgrade the venue or presentation.
